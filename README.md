### M. Ali Saghiri, MS, PhD

Biomedical engineer who builds health AI that has to prove itself against a reference standard. Working with neural networks since 2010.

**Now:** Associate Professor and Director of Research, Department of Restorative Dentistry, Rutgers School of Dental Medicine · Director, Biomaterials Research Laboratory

**Previously:** Philips Oral Healthcare (Innovation and Development) · University of Wisconsin–Madison · University of Michigan

**What I work on**
- Testing AI against physical and clinical reference standards. My first neural network located the minor apical foramen on radiographs correctly in 93% of test samples; in a cadaver study it was right for 96% of teeth, compared with 76% for experienced endodontists.
- Voice and other low-cost signals for diabetes and healthy aging.
- Evaluating large language models and AI agents in health education.
- Dental biomaterials and how diabetes changes tooth tissue.

**Code here**
- [`clinical-ai-proving-ground`](https://github.com/masaghiri/clinical-ai-proving-ground): toolkit and protocol for testing an AI tool against a reference standard.
- [`voice-biomarker-features`](https://github.com/masaghiri/voice-biomarker-features): acoustic voice features used in voice-and-diabetes research.
